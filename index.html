Measured notes on Arabic text handling, and the upstream fixes that came out of them. GitHub

  • 3,168 files use the broken Arabic recipe. Most of them are fine. 8 September 2026

    A code search finds the recipe in thousands of Python files, but whether it is a bug depends entirely on what draws the text. I read six of them: three were broken, three were correct, and one of those three does not even run.

  • The auto-fix for corrupted Arabic produces a real word. The wrong one. 7 September 2026

    Undoing reshape+bidi corruption round-trips exactly, until the text contains a lam-alef ligature. Then السلام comes back as السالم — a real word, a different word, one that survives a proofread.

  • Your Pillow version does not tell you whether Arabic will work 7 September 2026

    Shaping support is decided when the wheel is built, not by the version number. The same Pillow renders Arabic correctly on one machine and broken on another, and the correct code differs between them.

  • The Arabic fix everyone recommends is now the bug 3 September 2026

    The most-copied recipe for rendering Arabic in Python reverses your text on matplotlib 3.11, with no error. What happened when I filed it against Pillow and matplotlib.
